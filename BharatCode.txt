BuildProof is an evidence-backed student toolkit with three modes: defend a public repository, verify resume claims against one repository, and compare campus notices. It grounds reports in source lines and validates citations before display. BharatCode Qwen 3.8 27B is the primary model, with Google Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite as a server-side fallback. Submitted source code is never executed; resume and notice documents are processed transiently and are not stored in a database or hosted report.
